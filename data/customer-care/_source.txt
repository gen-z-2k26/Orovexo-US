3. Daily Order Cut-Off Time
Our daily order cut-off time is 4:00 PM Central Time (CT).
Orders placed before 4:00 PM CT on a regular business day are typically queued to begin processing on the same day.
Orders placed after the cut-off time will begin processing on the next available business day.
Orders submitted on Saturdays, Sundays, or recognized public holidays will also be queued for processing on the next business day.
Example:
* Order placed Monday at 2:00 PM CT: Typically begins processing Monday.
* Order placed Monday at 6:00 PM CT: Typically begins processing Tuesday.
* Order placed Saturday: Typically begins processing Monday, excluding holidays.
Our standard business hours are Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM Central Time.
Central Time automatically observes Central Standard Time (CST) or Central Daylight Time (CDT), depending on the season.

2. Reviewing the order for accuracy.
3. Confirming product availability.
4. Checking the shipping address.
5. Picking and inspecting the ordered items.
6. Packaging products securely for transportation.
7. Preparing shipping labels and documentation.

3. Conditions for an Eligible Return
To qualify for a standard change-of-mind return, the product must meet the following requirements:
